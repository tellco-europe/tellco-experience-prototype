The world needs 3× more renewable energy capacity by 2030 — and that transformation depends not only on technology and finance, but on trusted voices who move markets, shift perceptions, and inspire action. Partner Types

Sustainability Influencers

Reaching audiences who care about the planet

Climate, ESG, sustainable-living, and energy-access communicators who connect environmental issues with practical action.

PLATFORMSLinkedIn Articles · Instagram / Reels · Substack · YouTube · TikTok · Podcasts

Audience Profile

  • Climate-aware consumers and professionals
  • ESG and impact-investing communities
  • Corporate sustainability teams
  • Young professionals entering the green economy

Content Focus

  • Climate action and clean-energy adoption
  • Sustainable-living guidance
  • ESG and corporate-responsibility topics
  • Energy access and climate-justice stories
  • Community and environmental impact

Renewable Energy Influencers

Making clean-energy technology easier to understand

Solar, storage, smart-lighting, off-grid, and energy-efficiency educators who translate technical solutions into practical information.

PLATFORMSYouTube · LinkedIn · Technical Blogs · Webinars · Podcasts · Instagram

Audience Profile

  • Solar and clean-technology professionals
  • Engineers, installers, and contractors
  • Homeowners and business decision-makers
  • Energy-independence and off-grid communities

Content Focus

  • Solar and battery-storage education
  • Product demonstrations and comparisons
  • Off-grid and backup-power solutions
  • Smart lighting and energy efficiency
  • Technical explainers and project applications

Industry Thought Leaders

Content Focus
