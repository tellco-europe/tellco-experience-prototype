Mining Services

TellCo Brings Its Clean-Energy Platform To Mining Sites

Mine operators need infrastructure that can be installed, monitored, maintained, financed, and expanded around real site constraints. TellCo's mining offer builds on its broader clean-energy platform: solar, storage, lighting, water-energy planning, digital monitoring, EPC coordination, training, and long-term service management.

The result is a practical operating layer for remote and grid-constrained sites: power for critical loads, lighting for safe operations, asset visibility for logistics, and evidence for finance, ESG, and management reporting.
