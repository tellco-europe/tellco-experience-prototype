## SECTION 3 — One Ecosystem. Multiple Paths to Growth.

*Six platform pillars. Each area is a distinct content block with a title, short paragraph, and bullet points.*

---

### 3.1 — Partnership Opportunities

TellCoExperience XL brings together multiple partner pathways under one platform, making it straightforward for organizations to identify where they fit and how they can collaborate with TellCo Europe Group.

Partners may participate through:

- Product distribution and resale
- Technical installation and service delivery
- Project development and consulting
- Technology platform integration
- Financing and lending program collaboration
- Government and NGO program partnerships
- Affiliate representation and market advocacy
- Strategic market expansion and co-investment

---

### 3.2 — Revenue Streams

The program is designed to support practical, sustainable business growth. Partners can develop multiple revenue opportunities through a single partnership framework.

Revenue pathways include:

- Product sales and territory-based distribution agreements
- Project referrals and co-development fee arrangements
- Installation, commissioning, and ongoing service contracts
- Operation and maintenance (O&M) agreements
- Consulting, advisory, and technical support retainers
- Training delivery as an Accredited Training Partner
- Regional and country representation commissions
- Cross-division collaboration and co-marketing revenue

---

### 3.3 — Training and Certification

A strong partner ecosystem requires more than a commercial agreement. TellCoExperience XL provides a structured path for onboarding, product knowledge, compliance training, and capability development — so partners can represent TellCo solutions with confidence.

The certification framework progresses through four tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Registered Partner:** Platform onboarding, brand standards, and introductory product knowledge
- **Tier 2 — Certified Partner:** Product competency, delivery standards, and compliance by partner track
- **Tier 3 — Master Partner:** Advanced technical or commercial certification with access to exclusive program benefits and territory protections
- **Tier 4 — Accredited Training Partner:** Authorized to deliver TellCo training programs to third parties within an approved market

Training is delivered through self-paced online modules, live virtual sessions, in-country workshops, and an annual partner summit.

---

### 3.4 — Market Access

TellCoExperience XL helps partners connect to broader clean energy and infrastructure opportunities across regions, sectors, and TellCo divisions. A partner active in one area of the business may discover and access opportunities across the full ecosystem.

Market access spans:

- Emerging markets across sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and Southeast Asia
- Government electrification programs and public procurement frameworks
- Commercial and industrial (C&I) solar and distributed energy
- NGO and development organization programs
- Energy access and productive use energy deployments
- Project finance and development finance institution (DFI) co-investment
- Smart infrastructure and technology integration
- Cross-border and multi-country corporate energy programs

---

### 3.5 — Financing and Funding Pathways

Many clean energy and infrastructure projects face capital expenditure barriers that prevent viable opportunities from reaching implementation. TellCoExperience XL supports partners by connecting project opportunities with appropriate financing and funding pathways.

TellCo Europe Group is not acting as a lender. The platform facilitates access to relevant third-party financing instruments and connects partners and projects to suitable capital sources.

Financing support includes:

- Trade finance and inventory credit facilities for distribution partners
- Equipment financing and working capital structures for service partners
- Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) and lease-based capital structures
- Development finance institution (DFI) co-investment facilitation
- Blended finance structuring for public and development sector projects
- Results-based and output-based financing frameworks
- Market development fund (MDF) co-investment for demand generation
- Green lending and ESG-aligned financing product collaboration

---

### 3.6 — Growth Opportunities

The goal of TellCoExperience XL is to create a long-term growth platform — not a one-time transaction. As partners gain experience, complete training, register opportunities, and build capability, they are supported in expanding their business within the TellCo Europe Group ecosystem.

Growth pathways include:

- Advancing through certification tiers to unlock new benefits and exclusivities
- Expanding into new verticals within the TellCo ecosystem
- Entering new geographic markets with TellCo support
- Adding additional partner tracks alongside an existing partnership
- Participating in co-developed public sector and development programs
- Building toward Accredited Training Partner status to develop local ecosystems

Find Your Partner Track

**Entry point for:** Established businesses with existing distribution infrastructure, market presence, and commercial sales capability.

**Link:** Explore the Traditional Partner Track →

Sustainability creators, industry thought leaders, and brand ambassadors who translate clean energy topics into trusted narratives that reach investors, policymakers, procurement teams, and communities. Influencer Partners operate in both B2B and B2C spheres and are onboarded under a structured, compliance-backed engagement framework.

**Entry point for:** Content creators, sector experts, former practitioners, and advocacy professionals with established and engaged audiences in clean energy, sustainability, or infrastructure.

**Link:** Explore the Influencer Partner Track →

**Entry point for:** Licensed contractors, project delivery firms, O&M specialists, and service operators with proven clean energy installation or service delivery experience.

## SECTION 5 — Finance Connector

### Section Title

Finance Connector — Connecting Viable Projects to Appropriate Capital

### Overview

Many infrastructure and clean energy projects are commercially viable but fail to reach implementation due to capital expenditure barriers, currency risk, or unfamiliarity with available financing instruments. The TellCoExperience XL Finance Connector is designed to bridge this gap.

**TellCo Europe Group is not acting as a lender.** The Finance Connector is a facilitation service that helps partners and project sponsors identify, structure, and connect with third-party financing appropriate to their project type, partner track, and market.

### What the Finance Connector Supports

- **For Traditional Partners:** Access to trade finance, inventory credit, and end-customer financing products that reduce capital barriers for distributors and their buyers
- **For Service Partners:** Equipment financing, milestone-based project payment structures, and EaaS/LaaS capital arrangement support
- **For Strategic Partners:** DFI linkage facilitation, blended finance structuring, results-based financing frameworks, and co-investment coordination with multilateral and development capital sources
- **For All Partners:** Market development fund (MDF) co-investment eligibility for qualifying demand generation activities

## SECTION 8 — Partner Journey

### Section Title

The Partner Journey: Qualify → Train → Launch → Grow

### Overview

TellCoExperience XL follows a defined four-stage partner journey that takes an organization from initial application through to active partnership and long-term growth. Each stage has clear expectations, structured support, and defined outcomes.

---

### Stage 1 — Qualify

**What happens at this stage:**

The organization submits a partner application identifying their type, market, sector focus, and business background. The TellCo partner team reviews the application against current program availability in the applicant's market, assesses eligibility, and confirms the most appropriate partner track.

**Key activities:**

- Submit the partner application form
- Eligibility and market availability review
- Partner track assignment and initial commercial terms discussion
- Execution of the applicable partnership agreement

**Outcome:** Registered Partner status and platform access granted.

---

### Stage 2 — Train

**What happens at this stage:**

The partner completes the onboarding curriculum for their track, covering product knowledge, technical or commercial competency, brand standards, and compliance requirements. Training is delivered through the platform's online modules and supplemented by live sessions.

**Key activities:**

- Complete onboarding learning path for assigned partner track
- Product and systems knowledge modules
- Compliance, regulatory, and market-specific training
- Brand standards and communication guidelines
- Certification assessment and Tier 2 (Certified Partner) examination

**Outcome:** Certified Partner designation earned; full program benefits activated.

---

### Stage 3 — Launch

**What happens at this stage:**

The partner activates their commercial relationship with TellCo — registering deals and project opportunities, accessing co-marketing support, and beginning active engagement with the partner portal and relevant TellCo teams.

**Key activities:**

- Deal and project opportunity registration in the portal
- Activation of co-marketing agreements and market development fund access
- Introduction to TellCo's CRM and project pipeline workflow
- First customer or project engagement with TellCo support
- Access to Finance Connector for relevant project financing needs

**Outcome:** Active partner with live commercial engagement and verified market activity.

---

### Stage 4 — Grow

**What happens at this stage:**

As the partner builds a track record, they access opportunities to deepen and expand their engagement within the TellCo ecosystem. Growth is supported through advanced certification, new vertical access, expanded territory rights, and cross-division collaboration.

**Key activities:**

- Progress toward Tier 3 (Master Partner) or Tier 4 (Accredited Training Partner) certification
- Expand into new product verticals or service categories
- Apply for additional partner track recognition
- Participate in co-developed government or development sector programs
- Engage in annual partner summit and senior stakeholder reviews

**Outcome:** Expanded partner scope, increased program benefits, and long-term strategic relationship with TellCo Europe Group.
