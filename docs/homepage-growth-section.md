## One Ecosystem. Multiple Paths to Growth.

TellCoExperience XL is designed to help partners move beyond a single relationship, product line, or market opportunity. Through one global partner ecosystem, organizations can access structured pathways for collaboration, project development, training, certification, financing support, and long-term business growth.

Whether a partner enters through distribution, installation, consulting, technology, financing, advocacy, or public-sector collaboration, TellCoExperience XL creates a common framework to help partners qualify, train, launch, and grow across the wider TellCo Europe Group ecosystem.

### Partnership Opportunities

TellCoExperience XL brings together multiple partner pathways under one platform, making it easier for organizations to identify where they fit and how they can collaborate. Partners may participate through distribution, service delivery, project development, technology collaboration, financing support, government and NGO programs, affiliate growth, or strategic market expansion.

### Revenue Streams

The program is designed to support practical business growth. Partners can develop revenue opportunities through product sales, project referrals, installation and service work, consulting, training, co-marketing, regional representation, public-sector opportunities, and cross-division collaboration.

### Training and Certification

A strong partner ecosystem requires more than a commercial agreement. TellCoExperience XL provides a structured path for onboarding, training, certification, and capability development so partners can build the knowledge, credibility, and confidence needed to represent TellCo solutions effectively.

### Market Access

TellCoExperience XL helps partners connect to broader clean-energy and infrastructure opportunities across regions, sectors, and TellCo divisions. A partner that enters through one area of the business may discover opportunities in government projects, sustainable development, distributed energy, smart infrastructure, financing, or service delivery.

### Financing and Funding Pathways

Many infrastructure and clean-energy projects face capital expenditure barriers. TellCoExperience XL aims to support partners by connecting project opportunities with appropriate financing and funding pathways, helping partners move viable opportunities closer to implementation without positioning TellCo as a lender.

### Growth Opportunities

The goal of TellCoExperience XL is to create a long-term growth platform. As partners gain experience, complete training, register opportunities, and build capability, they can expand into new verticals, new regions, new partner tracks, and new project types within the TellCo Europe Group ecosystem.
