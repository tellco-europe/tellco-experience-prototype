Healthcare & Education

Reliable energy for essential services and learning environments

TellCo Europe supports hospitals, clinics, schools, colleges, universities, and training centers that require dependable electricity, efficient lighting, cooling, clean water, and resilient off-grid infrastructure. Solutions can be designed to keep medical equipment, communications, lighting, refrigeration, water systems, classrooms, laboratories, and administrative facilities operating where grid power is unreliable or unavailable.

TellCo Europe’s EAASI Africa initiative combines solar power and water kiosks for healthcare facilities, while its Indonesia initiative proposes tailored solar PV systems for schools, colleges, and universities. The CampSOL concept can also support clinic equipment, climate control, connectivity, lighting, refrigeration, and temporary training or healthcare facilities.

Relevant Solutions

  • Solar PV systems for hospitals, clinics, schools, and campuses
  • Battery storage and backup power
  • ELSOL electrical off-grid systems
  • ConSOL and CampSOL containerized infrastructure
  • Solar-powered water systems and water kiosks
  • Indoor and outdoor LED lighting
  • Solar cooling and refrigeration
  • Solar street and public-safety lighting around campuses
  • Energy audits, feasibility studies, and system design
  • Training and local technical-capacity development

Commercial & Industrial

Integrated energy solutions for productive and cost-efficient operations

TellCo Europe supports commercial and industrial organizations seeking to reduce energy costs, strengthen operational continuity, modernize facilities, and improve environmental performance. Solutions can combine solar generation, battery storage, efficient indoor and outdoor lighting, backup power, smart monitoring, engineering services, and energy-efficiency upgrades.

TellCo Europe serves a wide range of commercial and industrial clients, including manufacturers, warehouses, logistics companies, utilities, construction firms, and energy-intensive facilities. Support can cover the full project lifecycle, from feasibility studies and technical design to procurement, installation, monitoring, maintenance, and financing structures for qualified projects.

Water & Agriculture

Solar-powered infrastructure for water, irrigation, cooling, and food systems

TellCo Europe serves farms, agricultural businesses, rural communities, water organizations, and agrifood operators with renewable-energy systems designed to support water access, irrigation, cooling, storage, and remote operations. These solutions can reduce dependence on diesel-powered pumping and refrigeration while improving productivity and resilience in areas with weak or unavailable grid infrastructure.

The TellCo Europe portfolio includes solar-powered water pumps, solar water heaters, solar-powered cold-storage containers, solar cooling, off-grid power, and lighting for agricultural and rural environments. The Africa Solar Agrifood Processing initiative also aims to combine renewable energy and infrastructure to improve agricultural production, processing, storage, and distribution.

Relevant Solutions

  • Solar-powered water pumps
  • Irrigation systems
  • Solar water heating
  • Solar-powered cold-storage containers
  • Solar cooling and refrigeration
  • Solar PV and battery storage for farms
  • Off-grid ELSOL systems
  • ConSOL containerized power
  • Solar lighting for farms and rural facilities
  • Water kiosks and clean-water infrastructure
  • Energy for agrifood processing and distribution
  • Feasibility studies and agricultural energy assessments

Hospitality & Real Estate

Efficient, reliable energy for buildings, hotels, resorts, and developments

TellCo Europe supports hotels, resorts, residential developments, commercial buildings, property companies, villas, compounds, and real estate portfolios with solar, lighting, cooling, water, and energy-management solutions. Projects can be designed to reduce utility costs, improve guest and occupant comfort, enhance outdoor safety, and strengthen backup power for essential building systems.

Applications include rooftop or ground-mounted solar systems, battery storage, indoor LED retrofits, architectural and landscape lighting, solar park and pathway lights, solar water heating, cooling, backup power, smart-building controls, and EV charging. TellCo Europe’s Smart City materials also include smart homes and buildings as part of a broader energy-efficiency and utility-management approach.

Sports & Recreation

TellCo Europe provides lighting and supporting energy infrastructure for stadiums, football and soccer fields, tennis courts, sports parks, arenas, recreation areas, gyms, public parks, and other facilities that require reliable illumination, visibility, safety, and efficient operation.

Defense & Humanitarian

Rapidly deployable infrastructure for critical and remote environments

TellCo Europe provides modular solar, storage, lighting, cooling, water, connectivity, and containerized infrastructure for defense, emergency-response, humanitarian, disaster-relief, and remote operational environments. Systems can be configured to support temporary bases, field clinics, training facilities, accommodation, communications, refrigeration, lighting, security, and essential equipment where conventional infrastructure is unavailable or unreliable.

The CampSOL concept includes 20-foot and 40-foot configurable container systems with power generation, energy storage, climate control, remote monitoring, communications, CCTV, water systems, EV charging, and hybrid generator integration. TellCo Europe also identifies emergency power, temporary power, police, army, government, nonprofit, United Nations, and Red Cross organizations among potential users of its off-grid solutions.

  • Solar generators and battery-storage stations
  • Hybrid solar and generator systems

Mining & Manufacturing

Resilient energy for remote sites and demanding industrial operations

TellCo Europe supports mining companies, manufacturers, industrial facilities, warehouses, processing operations, and remote worksites that need reliable energy, efficient lighting, storage, monitoring, and reduced dependence on diesel generation. Systems can be scaled from backup power for essential operations to integrated solar, storage, lighting, cooling, water, and remote-site infrastructure.

TellCo Europe’s planned Chile initiative is intended to provide clean power to remote mining operations and nearby communities, with possible expansion into Peru, Bolivia, and Brazil under analysis. Relevant products include industrial high-bay lighting, floodlights, explosion-proof lighting, solar generators, battery storage, ConSOL systems, water pumping, low-voltage cables, and hybrid off-grid power.
