Partnership in Action

Collaborative Relationships Designed for Long-Term Value

TellCo Europe’s partnership approach brings together technical expertise, local participation, institutional support, financing capabilities and shared sustainable-development objectives.

EAASI Africa — Healthcare Electrification

This initiative delivers reliable solar power and clean-water access to healthcare facilities across Africa. By installing solar systems and integrated water kiosks, the program aims to help clinics and hospitals operate continuously while reducing dependence on diesel generators.

EAASI Indonesia — Educational Solar Initiative

This project aims to equip up to 1,000 schools, colleges, and universities in Indonesia with tailored solar photovoltaic systems. The program is designed to lower energy costs, reduce emissions, strengthen campus resilience, and give students direct exposure to clean-energy technology.

EAASI Cambodia — Tonle Sap Floating Villages

This project brings customized solar-energy solutions to the floating villages of Cambodia’s Tonle Sap Lake. It aims to provide cleaner and more affordable electricity, support local livelihoods and education, improve safety, and reduce environmental pressure on the surrounding ecosystem.

Kochi Canal Green-Gray Resilience Project — India

In partnership with the city of Kochi, this project aims to transform the city’s canal system through a combination of natural and engineered infrastructure. The initiative is intended to reduce flood risk, improve water quality, restore the urban ecosystem, and create more resilient public spaces.
